SALES ANALYSIS REPORT
=====================
Period: 02 Jan 2026 to 30 Sep 2026

Key metrics
- Total sales: $137,840 across 250 orders
- Average order value: $551.36
- Top product: Laptop ($51,000)

Findings
- Electronics is the top category with $95,460 (69% of sales).
- East is the strongest region ($48,010); West is the weakest ($27,885).
- Monthly sales peaked in 2026-06 ($22,690) and were lowest in 2026-05 ($7,655).
- The top 5 products generate 78% of total revenue.

Recommendations
- Keep inventory and marketing focus on Electronics.
- Investigate why West lags and run a regional promotion.
- Plan stock and campaigns around the 2026-06 peak.
